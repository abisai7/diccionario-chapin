### Definition

«Me llegas» is a Guatemalan colloquial expression used to show that you really like something or someone, find it excellent, or strongly agree with a person. It's a versatile phrase that can apply to objects, people, and situations alike.

The expression comes from the verb «llegar» (to arrive), but its meaning extends metaphorically to "reach the heart" or "hit the spot," indicating a positive connection.

### Uses

**To approve something:** When you like an idea, piece of clothing, or food. Example: «¡Me llega tu camiseta!» (I love your shirt!)

**To praise a friend:** When someone does something well or you really get along. Example: «Qué buena onda sos, me llegas.» (You're so cool, I love you.)

**To express support:** Shows you identify with someone's attitude or opinion. Example: «Me llega esa actitud.» (I'm with you on that attitude.)

### More examples

- «**Me llega** tu forma de pensar, cuate.» (I love your way of thinking, buddy.)
- «Esta comida **me llega**, está bien buena.» (This food hits the spot, it's really good.)
- «**Me llegas** vos, siempre estás cuando te necesito.» (I love you, you're always there when I need you.)
- «**Me llega** la música que pusiste.» (I love the music you put on.)
- «No **me llega** esa gente, son bien chismosos.» (I don't like those people, they're so gossipy.)
