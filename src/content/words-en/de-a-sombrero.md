### Definition

"De a sombrero" is a Guatemalan colloquial expression used to say that someone or something is excellent, doing great, or looking impeccable. It works as a positive qualifier that can be applied to people, appearances, results, or the good condition of any object.

### Usage

The expression is used to describe:

- **People**: "Mi papá es de a sombrero porque le gusta ayudar a los demás" (My dad is de a sombrero because he loves helping others)
- **Appearance**: "Andás de a sombrero con ese traje" (You look de a sombrero in that suit)
- **Results**: "El partido quedó de a sombrero, ganamos bien" (The match turned out de a sombrero, we won convincingly)
- **Objects**: "Tu carro está de a sombrero después de la reparación" (Your car is de a sombrero after the repair)

### Origin

The expression comes from the cultural association of the hat with dignity and good presentation: a person who "andaba de a sombrero" was someone well-groomed, respectable and of good reputation. Over time, the phrase broadened to describe anything considered good quality or in good condition.

### Cultural context

"De a sombrero" is a warm, complimentary phrase typical of everyday Guatemalan speech. It is used naturally in informal conversations between family and friends, and conveys sincere admiration without exaggeration.
