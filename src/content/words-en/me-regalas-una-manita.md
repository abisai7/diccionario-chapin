### Definition

«¿Me regalas una manita?» is a Guatemalan colloquial expression used to ask for help in a friendly and polite way. The phrase uses the verb «regalar» (to gift) instead of «dar» (to give) or «prestar» (to lend), which reflects a characteristically Guatemalan way of requesting favors.

«Manita» is the diminutive of «mano» (hand), metaphorically referring to someone's assistance or support. The diminutive gives the request a warm, lighthearted tone.

### Usage

Used in everyday situations where physical help or moral support is needed. Common among friends, family, and acquaintances, but can also be used with strangers in a polite tone. The phrase softens the request and makes it sound less demanding.

### More examples

- «¿Me regalás una manita to carry these bags?»
- «¿Me regalan una manita? I can't find the exit.»
- «Dude, ¿me regalás una manita? I got a flat tire.»
