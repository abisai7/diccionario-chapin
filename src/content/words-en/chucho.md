### Definition

«Chucho» is the most common colloquial word for dog in Guatemala, used far more than «perro» in everyday conversation. It is one of the most distinctive markers of Guatemalan Spanish. The word is also used to describe a stingy or miserly person who avoids spending money at all costs.

### Usage

As a synonym for "dog," «chucho» is used in virtually all informal contexts. It's the natural way to refer to a canine on the street, at home, or in the neighborhood.

As an adjective for "stingy," it's used to criticize someone who won't share or spend money. It can range from a lighthearted complaint to a serious critique, depending on context.
