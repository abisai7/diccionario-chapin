### Definition

The «pijije» is a whistling duck (family Anatidae) common in Guatemalan and Central American wetlands. In Western Guatemala, the word is also used colloquially as a mild insult for someone who acts foolishly or naively.

Unlike harsher insults, calling someone «pijije» has a gentler, almost affectionate tone and is often used among friends or family to gently correct or tease someone.

### Usage

Used in informal contexts to point out that someone made a careless mistake or lacked judgment. Commonly heard in departments like Quetzaltenango, Totonicapán, San Marcos, and Huehuetenango. The tone can range from playful among friends to a more serious scolding.

### More examples

- «That guy is such a pijije, he always forgets everything.»
- «Don't be a pijije, kid, you need to be careful.»
- «Pijije, you forgot to pay the electricity bill.»
